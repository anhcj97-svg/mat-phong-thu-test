Week 1 - Fiverr Official Seller Guidance (Tier A sources only; official Fiverr Help Center)

1. Creating a Fiverr Gig
FACT - Fiverr's official guidance
A Gig is the seller's storefront. Its main sections are: Overview (title, category, subcategory, search tags); Scope and pricing (packages, delivery time, revisions, price); Description and FAQ; Requirements (information needed from the buyer); Gallery; Publishing and review.
Source: Fiverr - Creating a Gig - checked October 31, 2026. https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010451397-Creating-a-Gig
Important official requirements:
- Title: clear, descriptive and client-focused
- Category: choose carefully; cannot change after publishing
- Search tags: up to five relevant tags
- Description: maximum 1,200 characters
- Packages: up to three
- Revisions: at least one revision option per package
- Requirements: collect information needed to complete the order
- Gallery: at least one image required; up to three images
Fiverr also recommends starting with one Gig and concentrating on it before expanding after positive feedback and multiple sales.
FACT - English requirement: The Gig description must be in English. Other languages can be added, but the information must also appear in English.
FACT - Media rights: Sellers must have permission to use uploaded images, videos and audio. Copyrighted material without the necessary rights can result in removal.
GPT's recommendation for Nhat Studio: Start with one coffee product photography Gig. Use a specific title that describes the service. Use all five relevant search tags. 2. Pricing packages
FACT - Fiverr's official package structure: up to three packages (Basic, Standard, Premium). Each package can have its own scope, revisions, delivery time and price. Buyers can compare packages and purchase the one that fits their needs.
Packages can also contain optional upgrades. Fiverr distinguishes: Upgrades = predefined additions available at checkout; Extras = separate optional services that can be purchased alongside the package.
Pricing rules (official guidance): Currency USD. General starting price $5. Category exceptions: some categories have higher minimums. Standardized categories may have mandatory minimum prices and deliverables. Service fee applies to all packages.
Important category check: Fiverr's standardized package documentation identifies a $15 minimum for its AI Artists category, among other category-specific minimums. 3. Delivery and order management
FACT - Fiverr's official order process: (1) Buyer places an order. (2) Buyer submits the required information. (3) Order enters In Progress. (4) Seller completes the work and submits delivery. (5) Buyer reviews, accepts or requests revisions. (6) Order becomes Complete.
Source: Fiverr - A freelancer's guide to the Fiverr order process - checked October 31, 2026. https://help.fiverr.com/hc/en-us/articles/360010639617-A-freelancer-s-guide-to-the-Fiverr-order-process
Delivery requirements - Fiverr instructs sellers to: check that the work meets the agreed scope; address the buyer's requirements; clarify missing or unclear information before delivery; check filenames, formats and file accessibility; include relevant completed files; explain what has been delivered in the delivery message. The seller must use the official Deliver Now process to submit completed work.
Important policy: Fiverr explicitly warns against submitting empty, incomplete or placeholder deliveries just to stop the countdown. Use Share Draft for work-in-progress previews instead.
Revisions and extensions: Revision requests depend on the package's included revision allowance and agreed scope. If additional time is necessary, the seller can request a delivery extension. The buyer has 48 hours to respond to an extension request; if the buyer does not respond within that period, the request is automatically accepted. After delivery, the buyer generally has three days to accept, request revisions or extend the review period.
Payment clearance: Fiverr's order-process guidance states that revenue generally becomes available 14 days after order completion. Seller Plus, Top Rated and Pro Talent freelancers have a seven-day clearance period under the documented rules. This is a clearance period, not a promise that a new seller can withdraw money immediately after completing an order.

Objective: Process a Fiverr order from receipt to completed delivery within a controlled 24-hour production cycle, without sacrificing product fidelity or commercial quality.

Two commitments: (a) 24-hour production target = internal workflow after all client requirements are complete and approved; (b) Fiverr delivery deadline = the actual deadline displayed on the order; we must always meet it, even if longer than 24 hours. The 24-hour target is initially an internal pilot, not a verified delivery guarantee.

1. Responsibilities
- Nhat: client messages, requirements, pricing, final approval - final decision
- GPT: generate still images, art direction, visual consistency - creative production
- MAT: coordinate production, video assembly, QC, exports and delivery preparation - production execution
GPT generates the approved still-image concepts. MAT handles video assembly and technical packaging. Nhat approves creative direction and final delivery. No one changes the client's approved product identity or adds unrequested elements.

2. Package production scope
BASIC $15: 1 still image; one product; one advertising scene; one final image; one included revision.
STANDARD $35: 3 still images; one product; three coordinated scenes; consistent product identity; two included revisions.
PREMIUM $60: 5 stills + video; one product; five coordinated advertising images; one 10-second video; two included revisions.
Proposed commercial deliverables; resolution, aspect ratio, video format, source-file inclusion and revision boundaries must be specified in the published Gig.

- Hour 20-24 Assembly and delivery (MAT + Nhat): organize files; prepare final ZIP if appropriate; check filenames and formats; Nhat confirms delivery message; MAT submits through Fiverr. Output: completed Fiverr delivery.

- Advertising direction: clean studio / white background; lifestyle advertising; luxury / cinematic; custom brand direction
- Aspect ratio: 1:1, 4:5, 9:16
- Client supplied exact packaging text / brand references
- Client confirms they have permission to provide and use the product images and brand materials
Additional required info: product dimensions and material; exact logo and label reference; background and prop preferences; lighting preferences if specified; intended platform and usage; video movement preferences for Premium; specific forbidden elements.
Intake rejection rule: if the source photograph cannot support accurate product representation, Nhat must request a better image or discuss a different scope before production. We do not silently reconstruct a defective label.

5. Visual production checklist
A. Product fidelity (mandatory, zero tolerance): product shape unchanged; original packaging proportions preserved; logo unchanged; label text unchanged; original product colors preserved; material appearance convincing; no invented packaging details; no distorted or duplicated product parts. If a generated scene cannot preserve the real product, stop and use a controlled compositing or editing approach. Do not redraw the product to hide the defect.
B. Commercial image quality: clear focal point; convincing contact shadow; consistent light direction; realistic reflections and material response; no unwanted objects; no random lettering or watermarks; no visible AI artifacts; correct aspect ratio; clean export at agreed resolution.
C. Video quality (Premium): exactly 10 seconds; product consistent throughout; smooth motion; no morphing or label distortion; intentional transitions; no unrequested text or music; correct orientation and export settings; plays start to end without errors.
D. Final file assembly: ORDER_XXXX/ -> 01_FINAL_IMAGES/ (image_01.png, image_02.png ...), 02_FINAL_VIDEO/ (product_ad_10s.mp4), DELIVERY_NOTES.txt. Only include folders relevant to the purchased package. Never include internal prompts, working drafts or source files unless part of the agreed deliverables.

6. Revision policy
Included revisions: Basic $15 = 1 round; Standard $35 = 2 rounds; Premium $60 = 2 rounds.
Qualifies: adjusting background brightness; minor lighting adjustments; small prop-position changes; minor composition corrections; correcting a defect against the approved brief; minor video timing or transition adjustments.
Does NOT qualify: changing to a completely different advertising concept; replacing the product with another product; additional images beyond the package; changing from lifestyle photography to a completely new campaign direction after approval; additional video scenes or longer video; complete redesign because the client changed their mind. These should be discussed as additional scope or a custom offer through Fiverr.
Revision procedure: (1) Client sends one consolidated revision request. (2) Nhat reviews and determines whether it is within scope. (3) GPT identifies the necessary visual corrections. (4) MAT executes and checks the revisions. (5) Nhat approves the revised deliverables. (6) MAT resubmits through Fiverr.
If we made a mistake against the approved brief, we correct it; we should not charge a revision merely to fix our own production error.
Fiverr's official guidance states the number of revisions depends on the Gig package or agreed scope; it recommends Share Draft for work-in-progress feedback and the formal delivery function only for completed work.
